How I work

What ties my work together is orchestrating AI agents: I set the goal, break it into pieces, direct the agents doing the work, and check what they produce. It's the same skill as running a product line, applied to a new kind of team.

At an Atlanta law firm, that approach produced SOPs and automations that cut the bill-pay process from about a day to minutes, and the lessons from that work became the foundation for Orbit.
