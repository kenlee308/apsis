Tello Follow-Me

An Android app that turns a standard DJI Tello into a follow-me camera drone, with all tracking done on the phone.

  • On-device ML Kit body (pose) and face tracking
  • Live H.264 video from the drone, decoded on the phone
  • PID steering with follow styles and distance lock
  • Photo and MP4 recording to the phone gallery, with optional phone mic
  • On-screen manual sticks and safety helpers (lost subject → hover, low-battery land)
  • Kotlin + Jetpack Compose
