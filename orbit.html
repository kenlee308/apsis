Orbit

Orbit is a local-first data and case-ops platform, starting with law firms. It brings cases, deadlines, letters and AI chat into one system, replacing spreadsheets and separate case-management software.

It grew out of my hands-on SOP and automation work at a personal-injury firm, where I saw how much time went into moving information between tools.

  • Cases, deadlines and letters in one place
  • AI chat over your own case data
  • Local-first: data stays on your machines
  • Runs on synthetic data for demos

Orbit is private. A live demo is available on request.
