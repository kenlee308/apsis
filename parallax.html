Parallax

Parallax is a local AI assistant that reads and fills forms in desktop apps. The idea: copy information, not pixels.

You box a region of the screen, such as a client table, and the AI turns it into structured fields in an editable Info Clipboard. Then you box a target, such as an intake form or a letter, and it decides what goes where and gives you the filled values or a rewritten letter.

  • Runs locally with Ollama models; nothing leaves the machine
  • Screen capture or image upload, with a paste-text fallback
  • Exact-name matches first, then AI mapping; low-confidence fields are flagged for review
  • Built with Next.js and TypeScript
